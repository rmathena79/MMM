PHASE 2: PREDICTION METHODOLOGY & BASELINE
================================================================================

Task 2.1: Define Seed-Based Baseline Model
The baseline expectation is that higher seeds beat lower seeds. However, upsets 
happen. Use the following seed matchup history as baseline:

- 1 vs 16: 1-seed expected to win ~99% of matchups
- 1 vs 8: 1-seed expected to win ~90% of matchups
- 2 vs 15: 2-seed expected to win ~95% of matchups
- 2 vs 7: 2-seed expected to win ~75% of matchups
- 3 vs 6: Approximate 65-70% for 3-seed
- 4 vs 5: Approximate 55-60% for 4-seed

Apply this as the default prediction unless evidence in Phase 2.2 overrides.

Task 2.2: Define Refinement Factors
Override or adjust the seed-based baseline when these conditions are met:

FACTOR A: Extreme Efficiency Mismatch
- If lower-seeded team has significantly higher KenPom rank (e.g., 10+ places), 
  increase upset probability
- Example: If a 6-seed is KenPom top-15 vs a 3-seed that is KenPom 25-30, 
  consider the upset viable

FACTOR B: Health/Injury Status
- Teams missing key players: reduce win probability by 5-15% depending on 
  player importance
- Known injuries (from bracket documents):
  * Duke: Point guard Foster (foot) - potentially out early rounds
  * North Carolina: Caleb Wilson (broken thumb)
  * Any other documented injuries noted in sources
- Healthy teams vs injured teams: favor healthy team

FACTOR C: Cinderella Indicators (applies mainly to double-digit seeds)
- High win total (28+ wins) with only tournament qualification preventing higher seed
- Recent winning streak (10+ games)
- Extreme efficiency rating despite lower seed (KenPom top 40)
- Examples: Miami (OH) 31-1, High Point 30-4, McNeese 28-5
- If cinderella meets 2-5 seed: still favor higher seed, but reduce margin slightly
- If cinderella meets another double-digit seed: consider toss-up

FACTOR D: Tournament Experience & Coaching
- Coaches with strong tournament records: slight boost for Elite Eight/Final Four 
  runs
- Teams making rare/first tournament appearances: slight probability reduction 
  (inexperience factor)
- Defending champion: slight home-court/confidence boost (e.g., Florida: +2-3%)

FACTOR E: Geographic/Site Proximity
- Home region advantage minimal in neutral-site tournament, but:
  * Houston team (Houston Cougars) in South Region played in Houston = true 
    home court = +3% boost
  * Ignore regional site location for all other teams

FACTOR F: First-Round Specific
- 1 vs 16: Essentially default to 1-seed unless severe injury to 1-seed
- 2 vs 15: Essentially default to 2-seed
- 3 vs 14: Default to 3-seed unless significant efficiency advantage for 14
- Avoid common first-round surprises unless data supports them

Task 2.3: Build Prediction Logic Framework
For each matchup:

1. Start with seed-based baseline prediction
2. Check each refinement factor (A-F)
3. Adjust prediction up/down based on factor evidence
4. For close calls (probability 45-55%): use tie-breaking rules
   * In ties: default to higher seed
   * In extreme cinderella cases: higher seed still favored but small margin

Task 2.4: Build Layered Predictions (Advanced)
After first round predictions are made:
- Use predictions from Round 1 to build Round 2 brackets
- Apply same refinement factors to higher-seed teams that advanced
- Account for team composition changes (fatigue, minor injuries from first games)
- Recognize that Elite Eight and Final Four predictions are less certain but 
  follow similar logic


================================================================================
PHASE 3: EXECUTION - GAME-BY-GAME PREDICTIONS
================================================================================

Workflow:
1. Parse the complete 64-team bracket into matchup pairs
2. For EACH of the 63 games (ordered by round):

   ROUND 1 (32 games) - First Round
   ROUND 2 (16 games) - Second Round / Sweet 16
   ROUND 3 (8 games) - Elite Eight / Regional Finals
   ROUND 4 (4 games) - Final Four / National Semifinals
   ROUND 5 (2 games) - Final Four
   ROUND 6 (1 game) - National Championship

3. For each game:
   a. Identify both teams
   b. Retrieve team stats, seedings, efficiency metrics
   c. Apply seed-based baseline
   d. Check/apply refinement factors A-F
   e. Make final prediction
   f. Log the prediction with reasoning (optional but recommended)
   g. Record to output list

4. Keep a running record of all 63 predictions in a single file
